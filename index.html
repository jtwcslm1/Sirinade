About Us

From family tables
to home kitchens.

Our story begins with a family Thai restaurant founded over 30 years ago. Through vibrant flavors and a modern approach, the restaurant introduced authentic Thai cooking to its community.

Over time, many customers shared the same challenge: while they loved Thai food, it felt difficult to recreate those flavors at home. Sirinade was created to solve that problem. Inspired by recipes developed in the restaurant kitchen, Sirinade brings together the key flavors of Thai cuisine into a single, easy-to-use marinade.
