Our Story

Our Story

We’ve been serving Thai food as a family for over 30 years!

We started as a small restaurant in San Francisco, built on a dream to redefine Thai food in the Bay Area. Over time, we saw how hard it can be to bring Thai flavors into a home kitchen.

Sirinade is a simple way to enjoy bold Thai flavor, without the guesswork.
